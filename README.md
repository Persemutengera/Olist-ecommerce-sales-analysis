#  Olist E-Commerce SQL Data Analysis
## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset using MySQL to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, seller performance, payment patterns, review scores, delivery performance, and customer geographic distribution.

The project demonstrates practical SQL and data analytics skills, including:

- Data exploration
- Data aggregation
- Filtering and sorting
- JOIN operations
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate functions
- Subqueries
- Date/time analysis
- Customer analysis
- Business KPI analysis
- Data-driven business insights

# Business Questions

The analysis answers the following questions:

1. What is the total payment value generated by the business?
2. How do sales change over time?
3. Which products have the highest sales volume?
4. Which product categories generate the most sales?
5. Which sellers generate the highest sales?
6. Which payment methods are most frequently used?
7. Which customers have the highest spending?
8. What is the distribution of customer review scores?
9. How long does delivery take on average?
10. Are orders delivered before or after their estimated delivery dates?
11. Where are customers located?
12. What are the key business KPIs?

# Dataset

The project uses the Brazilian Olist e-commerce public dataset, which contains information about orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and geographic information.

## Main tables
| Table	       |  Description                              |
|--------------|-------------------------------------------|
|customers	   | Customer information and location         |
|orders	       | Order status and order dates              |
|order_items	 | Products purchased in each order          |
|order_payments| Payment methods and payment values        |
|order_review  | Customer review scores and comments       |
|products      | Product information and categories        |
|sellers       | Seller information and location           |
|geolocation   | Brazilian ZIP-code geographic information |

# Analysis & Key Findings
## 1. Total Revenue

Total recorded payment value:

R$16,008,872.12

The calculation was based on the order_payments.payment_value column.

## 2. Monthly Sales Trends

Sales increased substantially from 2017 into 2018.

The strongest monthly revenue values in the available dataset included:

November 2017: R$1,194,882.80
April 2018: R$1,160,785.48
March 2018: R$1,159,652.12
May 2018: R$1,153,982.15

The final months of September and October 2018 contain relatively few records and should be treated as partial periods rather than interpreted as a complete decline in sales.

# Key Business Insights

##  1. Revenue and sales growth

The dataset shows substantial growth in monthly payment value from 2017 into 2018, with several months exceeding R$1 million in recorded payments.

##  2. Payment behavior

Credit cards were the dominant payment method, generating more than R$12.5 million in recorded payment value.

## 3. Product performance

High unit volume and high sales value were not always associated with the same products, highlighting the importance of analyzing both metrics.

## 4. Category performance

beleza_saude generated the highest sales value among the top 10 categories, while cama_mesa_banho recorded the highest unit volume among those categories.

## 5. Customer concentration

Customers were geographically concentrated in several major Brazilian states, particularly São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais.

## 6. Customer experience

The average review score was 4.09/5, while 5-star reviews represented 57.78% of all reviews.

## 7. Delivery performance

Average delivery time was 12.50 days, while the actual delivery date was on average 11.88 days earlier than the estimated delivery date.
