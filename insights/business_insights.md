# Olist E-Commerce Business Insights
## Revenue

The dataset contains R$16,008,872.12 in recorded payment value.

Monthly payment values increased considerably during 2017 and 2018, with several months exceeding R$1 million.

## Products and Categories

Product performance differs depending on whether performance is measured by units sold or sales value.

The highest-volume product among the top 10 sold 527 units, while another product in the top 10 generated the highest sales value of R$47,214.51.

At category level, beleza_saude generated R$1,258,681.34 in sales value among the top 10 categories.

## Sellers

The highest-selling seller among the top 10 generated R$229,472.63 in item sales.

Order volume and sales value do not always move together, making both metrics useful for seller analysis.

## Payments

Credit cards were the dominant payment method, with:

76,795 transactions
R$12,542,084.19 in payment value
R$163.32 average payment

Boleto was the second-largest payment method by payment value.

## Customers

The highest-spending customer generated R$13,664.08.

The analysis also shows that high spending can result from either multiple purchases or high-value individual purchases.

## Customer Reviews

The average review score was 4.09 out of 5.

Five-star reviews accounted for 57.78% of all reviews, while four-star reviews represented 19.29%.

## Delivery

Average delivery time was 12.50 days.

The average actual delivery date was 11.88 days before the estimated delivery date.

## Geographic Distribution

São Paulo had the largest customer population with 41,746 customers.

The top customer cities were São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Belo Horizonte, Brasília and Curitiba.
